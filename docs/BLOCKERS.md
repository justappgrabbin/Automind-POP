# Open blockers

- **Model Maker and developmental integration.** CPU TRIDENT training and
  revision activation have a bounded fixture test. Quantized TRIDENT export,
  attachment of newly trained heads, language-contact training, and conductor
  or embodiment promotion still require their own integration and evidence.
  The declared memory budget selects a model profile and does not enforce an
  operating-system memory cap.
- **Broader agent modes.** The mounted address mission uses the original one-shot
  coder with independent containers. Persistent interpreter sessions needed by
  step-by-step coding and the retrieval-assisted mission are pending. The
  original all-MiniLM-L6-v2 directory contains a placeholder. Retrieval modes
  need the embedding assets and their original ML dependencies.
- **Provider coverage.** Additional positional, Human Design, relational, and
  self-cultivation packages need provider-specific tests before mounting.
  Kimi and Foundry use different mandala intervals even at an independent
  astronomical position. Preserve their competing results pending a designated
  mapping authority.

- **ARLM Stage 2 and research qualification.** Stage 1 supports textual
  observations with declared lexical fixtures and the donor's adjacent-token
  graph. The five modalities can label those observations. Sensor processing,
  contextual truth evaluation, concept-driven addressing, ATO composition,
  and direct conditioning of TRIDENT on ARLM state need later integration.
  There is one pinned address and perspective pair per Stage 1 ledger.
  Quantum or ORBS research measurements, recipient heuristics, and the full
  five-experiment ARLM acceptance program remain open.
